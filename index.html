About

I started as a data analyst, building reports and answering questions for business teams. Over time the harder problems moved upstream: the data was late, duplicated, or impossible to trust, so I moved upstream with it and became the person who builds the pipelines.

Today I work on Microsoft Fabric and Azure, and I'm deliberately adding Purview to that so I can own both halves of the job: getting data to people, and making sure it's the right data. I've delivered 50+ projects for small and mid-size businesses, mostly on Upwork, alongside my engineering work.

I'm based in Bengaluru and open to full-time roles and selected freelance work.
